The Floor

One market session a day.
Drawn from real history.

Run a $100k virtual book through five decisions a day — every one a real historical episode. The March 2020 liquidity crunch. Wirecard. FX shocks. Make your calls, then the closing bell tells you what actually happened.

Conviction sizing

Stake 5–50% of your book on every call. Big calls pay big — and if you drop under $25k, you're margin-called.

Same session, worldwide

Everyone on Earth plays the same five decisions each day, so the weekly leaderboard is pure judgment — no luck of the draw.

Every session teaches

The recap shows what really happened in the market that day, and why — so even a losing day builds instinct.
